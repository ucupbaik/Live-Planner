# Live Planner Project Requirements

## Core Concept
A comprehensive web-based planner for live streamers and content creators to design their entire setup ecosystem.

## Visual Identity
- **Primary Color:** Blue (shades of blue for professional planning feel).
- **CTA/Special Buttons:** Rainbow gradients (representing creativity).
- **Theme:** Creative, technical but user-friendly.

## Screen List & Functionality

1. **Login Page**
   - Clean interface with "Sign in with Google" (Google OAuth) as the primary action.
   - Branding for "Live Planner".

2. **Dashboard (Home)**
   - Welcoming landing area with personalized greetings.
   - General information about the web app and quick stats.
   - Overview of recent projects.

3. **Main Navigation & Setup Hub**
   - Top navigation bar with account settings/profile.
   - Central selection menu for the 5 specialized rooms:
     - **Rakit PC (PC Builder):** Focus on internal PC components.
     - **Setup Software:** Configuration and software installation planning.
     - **Setup Live:** Streaming hardware, cameras, and lighting.
     - **Setup Studio:** Room layout, furniture, and acoustics.
     - **Setup Record:** Specific recording gear and audio chains.

## Project Interconnectivity
- Projects created in "Rakit PC" can be imported into "Setup Live" or "Setup Software".
- Assets and configurations are shared across the ecosystem.
- Each room has a unique UI tailored to its specific parts/category.
